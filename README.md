# DataFest 2026 — Patient Follow-Up Analysis

## What We Investigated

Using encounter, diagnosis, patient, and social determinants of health data, our team found a low rate of recorded follow-up after emergency department visits and hospital admissions. We investigated which patient groups were most affected and which factors were associated with delayed or unrecorded follow-up.

## Key Findings

Age and diagnosis features were among the most important predictors in our Random Forest model. This led us to compare follow-up patterns across age and diagnosis groups, where we found:

- Elderly patients with depression showed high rates of delayed or unrecorded follow-up.
- Patients with sepsis showed consistently high rates across age groups.
  
## Recommendations

- Book follow-up appointments before patients leave the hospital.
- Tailor follow-up outreach to different age groups.
- Explore home-based follow-up support for elderly patients with mental health conditions.
